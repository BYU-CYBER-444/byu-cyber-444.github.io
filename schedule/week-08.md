# Week 8 - Data Center Fundamentals
{: .no_toc }

---

## Topics

- Data center physical security: access control, mantrap design, CCTV placement, and tailgating detection
- Environmental monitoring: temperature and humidity, hot/cold aisle containment, and DCIM tools
- Power architecture: UPS types, PDU redundancy, generator sizing, and 2N+1 design
- Data center tiers: facility topology, maintainability, fault tolerance, and service-level implications
- Rack management, structured cabling standards (TIA-568), and colocation agreements
- Out-of-band hardware management: IPMI, iDRAC, iLO, and dedicated management networks
