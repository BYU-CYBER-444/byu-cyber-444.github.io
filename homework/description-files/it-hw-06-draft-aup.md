## 1. Purpose

This policy exists to make sure our computer systems are used properly and to protect the bank from misuse.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees who use Riverbend Community Bank computers.

## 3. Acceptable Use

Employees should use bank computers responsibly and only for work purposes. Reasonable personal use, such as checking a personal email account during a break, is generally fine as long as it doesn't interfere with work.

## 4. Prohibited Activities

Employees should not install unauthorized software, share their password with others, or use bank systems for illegal activities. Employees should not look up customer accounts they don't need for their job.

## 5. Monitoring

IT may monitor computer use as needed to ensure compliance with this policy.

## 6. Violations

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 7. Questions

Direct any questions about this policy to the IT Help Desk.
