The ungraded **practice midterm is available in Learning Suite**. Complete it as a review, then use any missed or uncertain questions to decide which topics to revisit.

## Study Guide

Use this checklist to organize your exam review. For each topic, be ready to explain what the concept does, why it matters, and how you would recognize or respond to a problem involving it. Review the course lectures and your notes as you work through the list.

### Infrastructure and virtualization

- Virtual machines, hypervisors, clusters, and shared storage
- How clusters maintain availability, including quorum and replication
- Secure access to management interfaces

### Operating systems and configuration

- Where system-wide settings are stored and how configuration changes affect services
- File permissions and how access-control lists provide more detailed access
- Approaches to distinguishing a configuration problem from a service or network problem

### Networking and name services

- How DNS resolvers and authoritative servers handle queries
- DNS caching, time to live (TTL), and why a record change may not appear immediately
- DNS security features and why recursion should be limited to intended clients

### Directory services and policy

- How directory structures organize users, computers, and policies
- How policy links and scope affect which settings can apply
- Why a linked policy may not be effective on every target, and how to verify effective settings
- How password policies can be applied to selected users or groups

### Identity and access management

- The common MFA factor categories and how they strengthen authentication
- Least privilege, account offboarding, and appropriate vendor access
- Risks of shared accounts and unnecessary permanent administrator rights

### Security controls and email security

- How security controls reduce system exposure and restrict risky behavior
- The purpose of email authentication and handling policies
- How to respond when a credential or secret may have been exposed, including rotation and review for use

### Certificates and public key infrastructure

- The roles of certificate authorities and why a root authority may be kept offline
- How certificate names identify the services clients connect to
- Certificate expiration, renewal planning, and revocation

### Data handling and compliance

- How data classification affects safeguards for storing, accessing, and sharing information
- What makes compliance evidence useful and auditable
- How to connect a policy requirement to evidence that demonstrates it is being followed

### Reliability, recovery, and operations

- Why a successful backup job does not by itself prove that data can be restored
- Recovery time and recovery point objectives, and the difference between downtime and acceptable data loss
- Redundancy, single points of failure, monitoring, and alert response
- Patch rollouts, change planning, rollback, and emergency changes
- Incident response and communicating status when the cause is not yet confirmed

### Data-center facilities

- How an uninterruptible power supply supports systems during power disruptions
- How power paths, device power supplies, and redundancy affect availability
- How airflow management supports cooling and what facility reliability tiers describe
