# Final Project Proposal
{: .no_toc }

---

## Overview

The proposal locks in which option you chose and what you will build before you invest significant time. It is graded pass/fail, and all five sections must be present and specific. A brief instructor feedback session in Week 9 lab will confirm your scope and your lab resources.

**Due:** End of Week 9. Submit your proposal as a single **PDF** in Learning Suite. The proposal is not submitted through a pull request, and it does not need to be in your portfolio repo. Pairs submit one PDF with both names on it.

{: .important }
The proposal is required. You may not receive credit for the final project if a passing proposal is not on file.

---

## Requirements

### 1. Option and Team

State which option you chose (for example "C4 - Compliance-as-Code Pipeline") and in one or two sentences why. Say whether you are working alone or in a pair. If in a pair, list both names and what each person owns. The project requirements are the same for one person or two.

### 2. Option Scope

Answer every item in the **Proposal Checklist** on your option's page. Answers must be specific to your plan (hostnames, tool names, numbers), not restatements of the option description.

### 3. Infrastructure and Resources

List every VM, container, or service you will run, with hostname, OS, role, planned IP, and approximate RAM and disk. Your list must include at least one Linux system and at least one Windows Server system, and state what the Windows system does in your project. Identify the heaviest component and how it fits your lab's RAM budget.

### 4. Tools

List the tools you will use for automation, scanning or testing, and logging or monitoring, and give one sentence of justification for each.

### 5. Week-by-Week Milestones

Give a personal timeline for Week 9 through Week 15 with specific deliverables each week (Week 13 is Thanksgiving break; no deliverable is needed). Generic entries such as "work on project" do not count. There are no graded checkpoints between the proposal and the final package, so your timeline is your own pacing plan: show what will exist by the end of Weeks 10, 11, 12, and 14.

---

## Grading

| Criterion | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Option and team | Option named; team and (for pairs) ownership stated | Option or team unclear |
| Option scope | Every item in the option's checklist answered specifically | Items missing or vague |
| Infrastructure and resources | Every component listed with role, IP, and resources; includes both Linux and Windows Server; fits the lab | Components missing or resources not considered |
| Tools | Tools listed with justification | Tools omitted or unjustified |
| Milestones | Specific weekly deliverables that build to a complete project by Week 15 | Generic or missing milestones |

A proposal that fails is returned with comments and must be resubmitted and pass before the project can receive credit.

Changing your option after your proposal passes requires instructor approval and a new proposal PDF.
